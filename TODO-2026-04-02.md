### Comprehensive Analysis (Calibration, ML, KL Divergence, Time-of-Day)

**1. CALIBRATION — Market is mostly efficient, but not everywhere**

Biggest mispricings (CI doesn't cross zero):
- ETH $0.65-0.70 @ SR 800-900: mkt=67%, actual=82.5%, **+15.5%** [+6.7%, +24.3%] (n=57)
- ETH $0.75-0.80 @ SR 600-700: mkt=77%, actual=90.8%, **+13.5%** [+5.8%, +19.6%] (n=65)
- SOL $0.60-0.65 @ SR 600-700: mkt=62%, actual=72.2%, **+10.1%** [+0.8%, +18.3%] (n=97)
- SOL $0.75-0.80 @ SR 600-700: mkt=77%, actual=88.3%, **+11.3%** [+3.0%, +18.0%] (n=60)
- SOL $0.40-0.50 @ SR 400-500: mkt=45%, actual=35.7%, **-9.3%** [-18.9%, -0.6%] (n=115)

Pattern: **The market consistently underprices contracts in the $0.60-0.80 range for ETH/SOL
at SR 600-700 (5-7 min into round).** YES resolves ~10% more often than the market implies.

But: "always buy YES" and "always buy NO" at SR 550-650 are both negative EV after fees.
The edge is in **selective entry** — only buying when price is in the mispriced bins.

**2. ML — Random Forest gets 75-86% accuracy but fees eat the edge**

- Top features: `mid` and `dist_from_strike` dominate (80-90% of importance combined)
- Momentum, volatility, and exchange divergence are near-zero importance
- AUC 0.75-0.93 depending on how late in the round (later = easier to predict but higher price)

Best ML results (positive or near-zero EV):
- **ETH @ sr300, 60% threshold: 369 trades, 88.3% WR, EV +$0.012 [-0.021, +0.042]**
- **ETH @ sr300, 65% threshold: 354 trades, 89.5% WR, EV +$0.013 [-0.019, +0.038]**
- **SOL @ sr600, 60% threshold: 314 trades, 80.6% WR, EV +$0.033 [-0.011, +0.077]**
- ETH @ sr450, 60% threshold: 350 trades, 83.4% WR, EV +$0.002 [-0.037, +0.039]
- SOL @ sr300, 60% threshold: 374 trades, 86.4% WR, EV +$0.001 [-0.032, +0.032]

**Key insight: the ML model is basically learning "distance from strike = outcome".**
The model doesn't find alpha beyond what the market price already reflects.
The one exception is SOL @ sr600 which shows +$0.033 — worth investigating further.

**3. KL DIVERGENCE — 5m market IS more informed than 15m for BTC**

- BTC divergence > 0.05: **follow 5m → 70.9% WR [68.4%, 73.4%] (n=1276)** ← STRONG
- ETH divergence > 0.05: 54.5% WR — barely above coin flip
- SOL divergence > 0.05: 49.6% WR — no signal

BTC 5m market is meaningfully more informed than BTC 15m when they diverge.
This is a different signal from cascade — it uses live pricing disagreement, not resolved outcomes.

**4. TIME-OF-DAY — SOL has wider overnight spreads, no directional edge**

- BTC: identical 1¢ spreads day and night
- ETH: identical 2¢ spreads day and night
- SOL: **1.5x wider overnight** (3¢ vs 2¢) — possible maker opportunity
- No consistent directional bias by hour for any coin
